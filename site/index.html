What the app does today.

The MVP focuses on the trusted manual loop: record, transcribe, inspect, summarize, export, and delete without a required cloud account.

Desktop recording with private artifacts

Capture microphone audio and feature-gated ScreenCaptureKit system audio into app-private WAV files with visible recovery and permission states.

Local Whisper transcription

Transcribe saved meetings using a user-provided local model path and keep channel-tagged transcript segments in SQLite.

Search and export

Find meetings with SQLite FTS5, inspect transcript history, export Markdown, JSON, and SRT, then delete private artifacts when needed.

Structured local summaries

Generate cited summaries, decisions, questions, and action items through a local Ollama setup, with hosted provider paths gated by explicit disclosure.
